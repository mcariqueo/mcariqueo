# Marcial Cariqueo Arriagada

**Clinical Pharmacist | PhD Candidate in Translational Medicine | PK/PD & Pharmacometrics | Critical Care Research**

I work at the intersection of clinical pharmacology, critical care, and data-driven translational research, with a focus on optimizing anti-infective therapy in severe respiratory infections.

My current work combines population pharmacokinetics, PK/PD modeling, therapeutic drug monitoring, real-world clinical data, and multicenter clinical research to support evidence-based decisions in complex patients.

## Current Focus

- PhD research in Translational Medicine at Universitat de Barcelona and IDIBAPS - Hospital Clinic de Barcelona.
- Population PK/PD modeling of anti-infectives in severe respiratory infections.
- Real-world evidence from pneumonia and respiratory infection cohorts.
- Clinical study coordination, patient screening, protocol implementation, and REDCap-based data workflows.
- Critical care pharmacotherapy, therapeutic drug monitoring, and model-informed dosing.

## Core Expertise

- **Pharmacometrics:** Population PK, PK/PD, model-informed drug development, therapeutic drug monitoring.
- **Clinical Research:** ICH-GCP, multicenter studies, study operations, clinical data quality control.
- **Healthcare Data Science:** Clinical data analysis, reproducible workflows, predictive modeling.
- **Therapeutic Areas:** Severe respiratory infections, pneumonia, anti-infectives, intensive care, vaccine effectiveness.
